# Everyday coaching redesign

User direction: engaging, interactive and understandable to an ordinary person; minimalism is no longer a constraint. Proceed without another design-choice round. Code-led native Android build.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal training-club companion: finish a useful action today, not configure an AI tool.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt, coral and mint on a cool light or ink-blue dark ground; Barlow Condensed bold headlines, readable system body type, generous Material controls. No fictional transformation scores or streaks.

STORY: Choose a focus, add a ready-made routine, complete one action, and explore clear answers when needed. Guidance works from installation; extra AI is optional.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A friendly greeting, a cobalt daily-plan panel with actual progress and one next action, then a readable action list or starter choices. Completion changes the panel and provides haptic feedback. Coach starts with interests and practical guides; a compact photo action brings answers sooner, with numeric photo detail collapsed.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3, training-club identity; other candidates were planner, style magazine, grooming studio, quests, wardrobe swatches and photo contact sheet. The roll selected candidate 3; seed: none (unavailable). The launcher supplied a short roll key rather than the documented full seed reference; raw evidence remains in the ignored local review folder. Boarding-board challenger declined: retain priority clarity without travel decoration. Design-annual challenger declined: retain disciplined type hierarchy without print costume. User's proceed instruction replaces another selection round. Native Material conventions and the user's ordinary-person usability request are the quality bar; no separate comp or quality-bar image was approved.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Native phone target; light/dark and 1.3× text checks. Font origin: Google Fonts Barlow Condensed, SIL OFL retained in app assets. No generated raster assets.
